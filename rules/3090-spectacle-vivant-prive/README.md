# Entreprises privées du spectacle vivant (IDCC 3090)

Version `2024.1`, applicable au **1er février 2024**, fondée on the extended salary agreement for the private live-performance sector and its official KALI article. The latest official salary text inspected on **October 11, 2026** is the 2025 agreement concerning the artists' and technicians' annexes; it does not replace the permanent administrative/commercial and technical minimums modeled here.

## Explicit legal scope

This model is intentionally bounded to the following recurring payroll rule:

- permanent employees of private live-performance companies;
- monthly gross minimums for the verified first echelon of seven administrative/commercial categories;
- monthly gross minimums for the verified technical categories, prorated by the contract work quotient.

The model does **not** calculate artists' fees, intermittent workers, touring or engagement-specific remuneration, annexes, seniority premiums, overtime/night/Sunday/public-holiday increases, meal/travel allowances, thirteenth month, profit-sharing, health, provident, training or branch-specific contributions. A classification outside the listed categories or administrative echelon 1 is deliberately reported as outside the modeled perimeter rather than inferred.
